\title{A fixpoint evaluator for positive datalog}
\author{David Wang}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
  A semantics supplement to the AFP \emph{Stratified\_Datalog} entry~\cite{datalogIsabelle}: a
  fuel-free fixpoint evaluator for positive datalog programs over a finite constant universe,
  together with two refinements of its inner loop --- assoc-list matching with a fact-driven body
  join, and an RBT-indexed join. The evaluator is an untrusted oracle in the intended use, so it is
  proved sound (every derived fact is derivable) but not complete.
\end{abstract}
